  • our vision

    To be the leading innovative, collaborative, and forward-focused nexus for research, knowledge production, and engagement on Africa’s place in the world.

  • our mission

    Use mixed concepts and methods to advance the understanding of Africa’s place in the world through research and publication, teaching and exchanges, public engagements and multimedia, archiving, and information storage.

pillars

what guides our work

critical african knowledge of the world

GEAR Hub is an African-based entity for critical thinking and analysis that produces cutting-edge knowledge and understanding of the world.

partnerships

Forging strategic partnerships with think tanks, organizations, and universities in Africa and the rest of the world to undertake collaborative projects and provide a base for visiting scholars researching and teaching in relevant areas.

knowledge centre

We are a vibrant home for collegial explorations between African institutions and their counterparts in the rest of the world to promote mutual understanding.

undertaking research

Consistent with our vision of research-active initiatives, GEAR Hub will engage in the production of research across political, economic and cultural spheres.

policy-relevant research

We engage in research that addresses pertinent policy issues in Africa’s relations with established and emerging powers and developing nations. GEAR Hub serves as a site for intellectual and policy exchanges, interactions, and partnerships.
